# Little Sprout / Little steps, big discoveries

## Treatment
16:9, 30 seconds. A tactile storybook product demo for families with Grade 1 learners. Promise: addition becomes something a child can do, revisit, and celebrate. Layout language: animated learning canvas, then close-up product theatre, then a family dashboard. Mood: warm illustrated storybook. Fredoka display and DM Sans supporting type; actual cream #fff9ef, green #397567, coral #ed7868, mint #cdebdc, butter #ffe7a9. Existing bunny illustration is the character. Motion language: counted object pops, apple drag trajectories, camera pullback, matched progress rings. The ending resolves the lesson's five steps into a brand lockup.

- Runtime: 30 s.
- Music: playful acoustic instrumental, marimba, pizzicato strings, soft brushed rhythm, 112 BPM. Clear accents near 4, 10, 15, 20, 26 seconds. Warm, curious, not frantic; resolved ending. No vocals.
