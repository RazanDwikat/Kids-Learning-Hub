## Build This Flow

Child Home
→ Grade 1
→ Mathematics
→ Addition
→ Start Lesson
→ 5 vertical educational videos
→ Interactive activities
→ Lesson completion

## Important

Keep the existing approved visual design and UX.

Do not redesign the application.

Implement the vertical short-form learning experience exactly as designed:

* Full-screen vertical videos
* Vertical scrolling/swiping
* One lesson with exactly 5 videos
* Progress indicator such as 1/5, 2/5, etc.
* No infinite scrolling
* After video 5, show the Lesson Complete screen

## Implement the 5 Videos

### Video 1

Animated rabbit story:

Rabbit has 2 apples.

Mother gives the rabbit 2 more apples.

Show the animation:

2 apples → +2 apples → 4 apples

Then:

**2 + 2 = 4**

Include:

* Character animation
* Object animation
* Voice narration
* Appropriate sound effects

### Video 2

Explain:

**Addition means putting groups together.**

Use animated apples and voice narration.

### Video 3

Interactive activity.

The child interacts with the apples and answers:

**How many apples are there altogether?**

Give immediate positive feedback.

### Video 4

Practice:

**3 + 2 = ?**

Use visual objects and answer choices.

Support:

* Correct answer state
* Incorrect answer state
* Friendly hint
* Voice feedback
* Animation

### Video 5

Final challenge:

**4 + 3 = ?**

After a correct answer, show a larger celebration and:

**🎉 Lesson Complete!**

## Audio

Implement working voice narration and sound effects.

Include a sound on/off control.

All visible text and narration should be in English.

## Interaction

The child should be able to:

* Swipe vertically between videos
* Interact with questions
* Receive immediate feedback
* Retry incorrect answers
* Complete the lesson

Do not make the experience a static mockup.

The interactions should actually work.
